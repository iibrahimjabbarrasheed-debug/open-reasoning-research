
# Open Reasoning Research

An independent open-source research project focused on building and evaluating intelligent reasoning systems.

## Vision

To explore how AI systems can understand problems, apply logical reasoning, analyze information, and develop structured solutions.

## Research Areas

- Logical Reasoning
- Mathematical Reasoning
- Critical Thinking
- Multi-step Problem Solving
- AI Evaluation and Benchmarking

## Goals

1. Develop transparent reasoning experiments.
2. Build reproducible evaluation datasets.
3. Explore methods for improving logical problem solving.
4. Publish useful open-source research tools.

## Project Status

Early-stage research project. Development and experiments are in progress. No trained AI model is currently being claimed or released.

## Contributions

Contributions, research ideas, and constructive feedback are welcome.
